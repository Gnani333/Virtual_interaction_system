🔄 AUTOMATIC CONFLICT RESOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRIORITY ORDER:
  1️⃣  Voice   (Highest Priority) - Always wins
  2️⃣  Gesture (Medium Priority)   - Blocks eye
  3️⃣  Eye     (Lowest Priority)   - Blocked by others

EXAMPLE:
  If you speak "Copy" while:
    • Making a peace sign → Voice wins, gesture ignored
    • Looking right       → Voice wins, eye tracking ignored
    • Both gestures       → Gesture cooldown enforced

🛠️ CUSTOMIZATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

To customize:
  1. Open config.py
  2. Edit the relevant _CONFIG dictionary
  3. Restart application

Example - Increase eye tracking sensitivity:
  EYE_CONFIG["gaze_hold_time"] = 0.5  (instead of 0.8)

Example - Increase gesture detection stability:
  GESTURE_CONFIG["gesture_frames_required"] = 5  (instead of 2)

Example - Adjust voice microphone sensitivity:
  VOICE_CONFIG["energy_threshold"] = 3000  (instead of 4000)
